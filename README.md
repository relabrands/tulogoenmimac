# Mac Brand Spaces

Build a modern, minimalist landing page inspired by "brandmymac.com" for a tech founder in the Dominican Republic who is selling spots on their MacBook for company logos and brand sponsorships. 

Design and Layout Guidelines:
- Aesthetic: Clean, modern, Apple-inspired monochrome style (white, light grays, deep blacks, subtle borders, sharp typography).
- Header: A bold title "Your brand, on my Mac.", followed by a subtitle: "Your logo travels with me on a founder's best friend: the MacBook."
- Progress Bar: A prominent funding/progress bar showing total raised (e.g., "RD$15,280 raised • goal passed 142%"), with text showing remaining spots or price starting point (e.g., "Spots from RD$1,000").
- Live Activity Feed: A ticker/notification bar at the top or bottom showing recent purchases (e.g., "StartupRD took the top-left lid spot for RD$1,500 - 2h ago").

Interactive Mac Selector & Views:
- Provide toggle buttons for "Lid" (Outer cover) and "Inside" (Keyboard / Palm rest area), exactly like brandmymac.com.
- Interactive Grid/Mockup: Display a visual representation of the MacBook surface divided into distinct grid slots (dashed borders for available spots, solid filled logos with prices for taken spots).
- Hover/Click Interaction: When clicking an available spot, open a clean modal or sidebar to "Claim this spot" (simulate buying/reserving it).
- Show sample logos (like tech companies, local startups, funny/meme placeholders) in the already-taken spots to make it look active and populated.

Extra Sections:
- Pricing Tiers / Grid of slots with clear prices in Dominican Pesos (RD$).
- A section explaining "How it works" (1. Pick a spot, 2. Upload your logo & link, 3. Get seen online & IRL).
- Footer with copyright and social links.

Make it fully responsive, interactive, using React, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide icons for a sleek, production-ready frontend look.
